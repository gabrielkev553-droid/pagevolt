Hi, I'm a 13-year-old developer from India and one of the youngest ai startup founders in the world. My 14th birthday is on October 24th, 2026. My personal mission is to completely build, code, and launch a working software product before that exact date.

I don't have a laptop or computer yet, so I am vibe coding the entirety of PageVolt using Google IDX and a free Gemini API directly inside the Chrome browser of my Android tablet. I noticed how busy creators and founders waste hours on Canva trying to format carousels for LinkedIn, Instagram, and YouTube. PageVolt is my solution to automate that entire layout chore in seconds so you can spend your time building instead.

I am doing web design services on the side to fund my first MacBook, but my main focus is launching this app. I'm building this entirely in public. If you want to follow my daily updates, code fixes, and startup journey, connect with me!
